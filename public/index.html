The Opportunity Seeker

A problem is more than something that needs fixing. It's an opportunity to create value.

The entrepreneurial instinct is to look at an everyday frustration and immediately ask two questions: How can this be solved, and how can solving it become a business opportunity?

From e-commerce returns to wasted bakery inventory and the challenges real-estate professionals face, the goal is to connect real problems with practical solutions.
